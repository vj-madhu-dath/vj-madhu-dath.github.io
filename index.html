01 · Music

Flute Timer

A practice timer that listens. Starts when you play, logs the silence in between, and keeps both hands on the flute. 2026 · Browser · No account Open app
Does it record audio?

No. The browser measures live loudness and discards the audio. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Does it need a mic?

Yes. Allow microphone access in your browser so the timer can detect when sound starts and stops.

Can it identify notes?

No. It measures sound level, not pitch, tuning, or whether you played the right note.

Other instruments?

Yes. Any sustained sound can start a play lap. Calibrate in the room you use and adjust sensitivity if needed.

Does it save sessions?

The current session stays in the open tab. Settings and room calibration are saved in this browser.

02 · Photography

Camera Butler

Find your better angle. Live framing guides, a level horizon, and on-device object detection for better photos. 2026 · Browser · On-device AI Open app
Are photos uploaded?

No. Camera frames are processed in your browser. A capture becomes a file only if you choose to download it.

Try it without camera?

Yes. The illustrated demo works without granting camera access.

What guidance?

It offers angle and composition prompts, a grid, a rough brightness reading, and an optional horizon sensor on supported devices.

Is object detection required?

No. Object detection is optional. The framing prompts and camera tools work without it.

Can it judge a photo?

No. It offers suggestions, not a verdict. You decide whether the composition works for your picture.

03 · Productivity

DoneDoner

Keep a record of what you finish. A small done list and scratchpad, saved in this browser, with CSV and PNG exports. 2026 · Browser · Local storage Open app
What is DoneDoner?

A simple list for completed tasks, with a separate scratchpad for loose notes.

Where are tasks saved?

Your tasks and notes stay in this browser’s local storage, in this browser profile and site.

Does it sync devices?

No. DoneDoner has no account or sync service, so another browser or device has a separate list.

What do exports contain?

CSV includes tasks, status, and created date. PNG captures the visible done list. The scratchpad is not included.

Can I restore an export?

Not currently. Exports are copies for your records; the app does not import or restore them.

04 · Business

Deadline Calendar

Never miss a filing date. A short profile turns into every federal and California deadline for the year — as a calendar file and a printable checklist. 2026 · Browser · No account Open app
What does it generate?

It builds a twelve-month list from a short business profile, with dates grouped by month.

Which places are covered?

It covers US federal and California dates. It does not include rules for other states.

Can I export dates?

Yes. Export an .ics calendar file or print a checklist with the sources attached.

Where is my profile saved?

Your profile stays in local storage in your browser. There is no app account or backend.

Is it official advice?

No. It is a calendar, not a tax preparer. Check each date against its linked agency source.
